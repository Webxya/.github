# Webxya

**Building accessible digital solutions for global impact.**

We work with NGOs and purpose-driven organizations to create inclusive, accessible, and effective digital experiences.

## What we do

- **Web design & development**: accessible, secure, and scalable websites built to support real-world impact.
- **Accessibility & inclusive design**: WCAG-aligned digital experiences designed for everyone, from the start.
- **Digital strategy & consulting**: clear guidance to align technology, accessibility, and organizational goals.
- **Visual identity & communication design**: clear, inclusive, and consistent design for digital and print communication.
- **AI-assisted solutions**: responsible use of AI to enhance planning, content, and digital workflows.
